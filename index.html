About Tech Team India

Tech Team India is a technology community initiative founded by Sharan Kumar with the goal of creating a centralized ecosystem where students, developers, organizers, communities and technology-focused opportunities can connect.

Tech Team India focuses on making technology opportunities easier to discover by bringing hackathons, CTFs, workshops, competitions and community initiatives together through a connected multi-channel ecosystem.
